The P3/P4 pre-hardware and hardware-acceptance rules are preserved only for replay and audit of those stages. They do not authorize a new run and do not govern P8 or later work.

- [`docs/P3_PRE_HW_ACCEPTANCE_PACKAGE.md`](../P3_PRE_HW_ACCEPTANCE_PACKAGE.md)
- [`docs/P4_PRE_HW_ACCEPTANCE_PACKAGE.md`](../P4_PRE_HW_ACCEPTANCE_PACKAGE.md)

Their generic `HARDWARE_ACCEPTANCE: PENDING_HW` markers describe the historical stage or an offline invocation, not the current canonical P7 scoped result. Current status comes only from `config/project_state.json` and the generated root `PROJECT_STATUS.md`.
